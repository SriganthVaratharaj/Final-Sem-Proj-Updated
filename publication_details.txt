C. PUBLICATION:

The project titled “Multimodal Document Intelligence System for Invoice and Receipt Processing Using Vision-Language Models with Layout-Aware OCR” by Dr. P. Nandhini, Sriganth G V, Jayasimbu J, Aadhiseshan S, and Remo V has been submitted and is currently under consideration for publication in the International Journal on Document Analysis and Recognition (IJDAR) published by Springer Nature.

The project focuses on developing an OCR-free, pixel-level visual understanding system for complex financial documents using the Qwen2.5-VL-32B model, utilizing local CLAHE preprocessing, dynamic overlap splitting, and Cloudflare-tunnelled remote GPU workers. The system was developed using Python, React.js, FastAPI, MongoDB, and Llama.cpp.
